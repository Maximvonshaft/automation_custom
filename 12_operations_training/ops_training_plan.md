# Operations Training Plan

## Training Objective

Operators should understand:
- what changed,
- how to perform the workflow,
- what errors mean,
- when to escalate,
- how to confirm completion.

## Training Materials

| Material | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|
| SOP | Ops owner | Draft |
| Screenshots | Product/QA | Draft |
| FAQ | Support | Draft |
| Escalation guide | Support lead | Draft |

## Training Checklist

- [ ] Workflow demonstrated.
- [ ] Normal path covered.
- [ ] Exception path covered.
- [ ] Escalation path covered.
- [ ] Known limitations explained.
