# External Distribution Policy

Do not distribute:

- source repository;
- PowerShell/Python green ZIP;
- country packs;
- coordinate maps;
- dropdown rules;
- business mappings;
- SafeBrake policies;
- real customer templates or rows.

External operator receives only:

- compiled Agent later;
- tenant/machine license;
- signed short-lived job plans;
- operator guide;
- evidence output access.
