# Business Context

The commercial product is not an ASYCUDA script. It is a controlled customs automation service for legacy customs systems where APIs, XML import, JAB, selectors, or system cooperation are unavailable.

The operator-facing workflow is intentionally simple: drag a fixed Excel template into the software and start work. The valuable intellectual property—field coordinates, dropdown strategy, business mapping, SafeBrake policy, and error handling—must stay under HQ control.

## Commercial value

- Prevent local agents or IT vendors from copying a green ZIP tool.
- Reduce manual customs data entry time.
- Standardize country-specific customs knowledge into server-side packs.
- Provide auditable evidence bundles for every run.
- Support per-tenant, per-machine, per-country-pack monetization.
