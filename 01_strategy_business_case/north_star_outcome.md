# North Star Outcome

A field operator can run ASYCUDA automation on an authorized machine, but cannot extract, copy, or independently reuse the Albania ASYCUDA automation know-how.

HQ can:

- issue and revoke machine licenses;
- compile signed one-time job plans;
- keep Country Packs server-side;
- collect evidence bundles;
- audit every run;
- disable unsafe modes;
- scale the same runtime to additional countries.
