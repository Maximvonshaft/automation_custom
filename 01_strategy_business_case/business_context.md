# Business Context

The team has proven that ASYCUDA Line Transaction fields can be driven through blackbox foreground automation. The current internal tool is commercially valuable but unsafe to distribute because it contains executable know-how and country-specific configuration.

## Business problem

Local agents, customs brokers, or IT vendors may attempt to copy the automation logic, reverse engineer the field coordinates, or reproduce the Country Pack. If the system remains a portable ZIP, the core business asset is exposed.

## Business objective

Turn the internal tool into a controlled automation service where Speedaf/HQ owns the automation rules, job generation, licensing, evidence, and revocation.

## Commercial outcome

- Preserve IP and operational know-how.
- Enable per-tenant / per-machine / per-country licensing.
- Support paid country pack subscriptions.
- Enable usage metering and evidence-based billing.
- Prevent unauthorized reuse by local IT vendors.
