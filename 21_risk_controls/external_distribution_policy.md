# External Distribution Policy

Do not send external parties:

- source code
- PowerShell scripts with full execution logic
- full Country Pack
- coordinate maps
- dropdown maps
- business mapping Excel or YAML
- internal review bundles without watermark/sanitization

Allowed external deliverables:

- signed Agent installer
- operator guide
- tenant account
- time-limited signed job plans
- sanitized evidence reports

Any request by a local IT company for source/config access must be escalated to HQ.
